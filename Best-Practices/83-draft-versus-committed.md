# Draft versus committed

A draft is not the committed fact. Readers of the committed fact do not see the draft unless the contract says they do.

No current official page was opened for this rule. Confirm a primary source before treating it as tested.

Discarding a draft leaves the committed fact unchanged. Publishing a draft is the write that changes it. See [Single writer](45-single-writer.md).

## Check

Save a draft and read as a different caller who can only see committed facts. The draft is absent.
